# Ozmoo Apple IIgs font: 6x8 in an 8-wide cell.
#
# The 640-mode reference font (8 pixels per character cell at 80 columns), and
# the master the antialiased 320-mode font is derived from by averaging column
# pairs.  Glyphs live in columns 0-5; columns 6 and 7 are always blank, so the
# derived 4-sample glyph is 3 samples of ink plus a blank one.
#
# Stems are one pixel wide on purpose.  Making them two would land every feature
# on an even boundary and the antialiased derivation would then be identical to
# a hand-drawn 3-wide font, which is the thing it is being compared against.
#
# Baseline is row 6; row 7 is blank except for descenders.  Drawn for Ozmoo,
# September 2026 - not derived from any ROM font.
#
# Format: @<hex code>, then 8 rows of 6 columns, '#' ink and '.' paper.
